# Project Title: AI-Assisted Personal Investment Research & Decision Support System

## Project Description 
### 1. Introduction
#### 1.1 Personal Motivation and Background
- This project is motivated by my own experience as an individual investor. I regularly follow a small group of public companies and need to read financial results, company announcements, news, and other public information. The main difficulty is not a lack of financial data. Existing services already provide much of the basic market and company information I need. The difficulty is organizing that information around my own investment reasoning and keeping that reasoning consistent over time.
- For example, when I follow a company, I may have an initial view about its growth drivers, risks, and valuation. A new earnings release or company announcement may support that view, weaken it, or have little effect. Today, this process is handled through a mixture of web pages, notes, spreadsheets, and memory. I want to build a small system that makes this personal research process more organized and easier to review later.
- The project therefore does not aim to reproduce an investment website such as Yahoo Finance. Basic financial information can be reused from existing sources such as yfinance, company investor-relations pages, and public filings. The project focuses on the additional layer that is useful to me personally: connecting new evidence with the investment reasoning I have already recorded.
#### 1.2 Problem Definition
- The problem is a practical workflow problem: how can an individual investor organize company research so that new information can be reviewed against an existing investment thesis, while preserving a clear record of why the thesis or selected assumptions changed?
- The proposed system will provide a small, focused workflow for this purpose. It will not attempt to predict stock prices, make autonomous Buy/Sell decisions, or replace professional financial-data services. Its purpose is to help me perform and document my own investment analysis more consistently.
